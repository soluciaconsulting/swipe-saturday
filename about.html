About Swipe Saturday

Built by a storyteller, run like a studio.

We believe every business — no matter the size — deserves content that performs, not just content that exists.

Our Mission

Content that gets businesses seen — and remembered.

Swipe Saturday started with a camera, a car, and a belief that great content shouldn't be reserved for national brands with national budgets. Today we help retailers, breweries, farms and destinations across the Midwest tell their story in a way that actually reaches people — and moves them to act.

Every project is treated like a partnership: we learn the business first, then build the content strategy around it — not the other way around.

Our Story

How we got here

2021

The first camera, the first client

Swipe Saturday launched shooting short-form content for local Des Moines small businesses on nights and weekends.

2022

FAA Part 107 certification

Earned our Remote Pilot Certificate and added commercial drone cinematography to every project offering.

2023

First retail & retainer clients

Began working with regional retailers on ongoing monthly content partnerships instead of one-off shoots.

2024–2025

Destination & event work across the Midwest

Expanded into destination marketing and live event coverage, growing an engaged audience of 17K+ on Instagram.
